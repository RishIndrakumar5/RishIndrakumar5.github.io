About Me

My name is Rishwanth Indrakumar and I am studying in the 8th grade at CREC Academy of Computer Science and Engineering Middle School. I love building, gaming, and chess. I also do debate at CTMSDL, and I played 2nd violin in the CMEA regional orchestra.

I'm always curious about how things work and enjoy taking on challenges that push me to think creatively. Whether I'm constructing something with Legos, strategizing in a chess game, or building projects that solve real problems, I bring enthusiasm and dedication to everything I do.

My passion for design, engineering, and problem-solving drives me to explore new ideas and create innovative solutions. I aspire to become an architect, and I believe that with creativity and determination, we can build amazing things that impact the world around us.
